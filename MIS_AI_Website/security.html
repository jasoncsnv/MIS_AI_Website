Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack spread rapidly across computer systems worldwide. This malware encrypted important files and demanded payment from victims to regain access to their data. The attack disrupted hospitals, businesses, and government organizations, highlighting the importance of updating software and protecting systems against cyber threats.
