Correct AI Usage

AI should be used to help college students understand difficult topics, rather than doing all of their thinking for them.
AI also should be used to accerrelate learning, such as explaining lessons, offering practice questions, and suggesting ways to improve a drafted essay.
Students should also make sure that information they read isn't AI-generated because it could be false or misleading.
When using AI, students should They should abide by their professor's rules and acknowledge AI support.

AI Evaluation and Critique

I agree that using AI as a learning tool is more responsible than submitting completely AI-generated work and claiming it as one's own.
A useful addition is to protect personal information by avoiding uploading private institutional or workplace data.
Students should also read over reliable sources to verify claims or facts.
Whether AI is permitted depends on the person in charge, and it is important to follow those rules.

Benefits of AI

  1. One major benefit of AI is that it can explain complex ideas to learners in simpler ways.
  2. Another benefit is that AI can create specialized learning tools and plans that best fit with the user.

Who Benefits?

  1. Students who want extra academic support and learning support benefit from AI assistance.
  2. Employees who work on projects that need help brainstorming or improving workflows can also benefit from AI.

How AI Tools Used

  • Students can use AI to generate practice quizzes and study plans.
  • Employees can create project timelines and deadlines with AI assistance.

Incorrect AI Usage

Three Incorrect Ways to Use AI

Academic Dishonesty
Submitting fully AI-written answers and claiming it as original work when not allowed.

Sharing Private Information
Importing personal details about someone or confidential documents into an AI tool without given permission.

Using Unverified Answers
Using incorrect or misleading facts or citations from AI without checking verified sources first.

I think AI is useful, but honesty must be shown when students work on assignments. It is unfair to present fully AI-generated work and claiming it as a personal effort when it is forbidden. Relying on AI without fact-checking lead to misleading and incorrect information. Students should use AI responsibly while making sure their claimed work is from their own learning and effort.
